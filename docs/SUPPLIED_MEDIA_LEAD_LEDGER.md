## Public Design Findings

The published homepage was reviewed in Arabic and English on 24 August 2026. The **English hero** is the stronger reference point: image-led, asymmetrical, calm, and legible. The Arabic experience preserves the intended mirrored reading direction. The refinement should therefore retain the current hero’s bilingual editorial mechanics, simplify the density of secondary sections, and elevate card details rather than replacing the established system.

The following boundaries remain non-negotiable:

1. A visible portrait and a separate product render must not be interpreted as a confirmed watch pairing.
2. Watermarks and publisher logos must never be removed, hidden, or overwritten.
3. The public archive must distinguish **observed appearance**, **maison-confirmed model information**, and **unverified lead**.
4. Full-resolution gallery media requires an original or rights-cleared image plus a documented mapping to an exact archive record.
