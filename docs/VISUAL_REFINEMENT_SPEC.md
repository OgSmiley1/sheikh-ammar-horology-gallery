# Visual Refinement Specification

## Intent

This refinement deliberately **does not replace** the established atelier system. The English hero’s image-led study and the Arabic mirrored experience are the correct visual baseline. The work therefore keeps the ivory, mineral ink, nocturne olive, and burnished bronze system; the Cormorant/Arabic typographic pairing; the source-bound story hero; and the protected bilingual shell.

The Collection route becomes quieter and more precise. It is reframed as an **archive atlas**, not a complete private inventory. Classification moves from a floating image badge into the card’s reading order. The image plane receives more space, the filter surface reads as a considered editorial index, and the collection counts describe **published records** rather than a claim of ownership or completeness.

## Preserve

| Element | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage watch-story sequence | Retain | It is the strongest image-led entry point and maintains explicit source boundaries. |
| English/Arabic composition | Retain and mirror | English leads image-left/copy-right; Arabic preserves the inverse editorial reading direction where the layout splits. |
| Shared header and language control | Retain | The current compact maison signature and `xl` navigation transition remain functional and accessible. |
| Archive film and public references | Retain | These are clearly distinguished from the archive’s own source record. |
| Signed administrator session and public-route cache policy | Preserve untouched | Visual work must not loosen security or deployment safeguards. |

## Refine

| Surface | Change | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Collection title and metrics | Use archive-record language and exact live counts | Removes any implication of a complete personal inventory. |
| Watch cards | Move classification from the image overlay into the textual card hierarchy | Makes images calmer and improves scanability. |
| Card image plane | Increase whitespace and add a subtle mineral radial treatment | Gives the object room without adding visual noise. |
| Filter surface | Use a single table-like editorial field rather than a generic control panel | Reinforces the research-archive character. |
| User-supplied composites | Keep outside the public visual library | They remain research leads until provenance, rights, and watch association are established. |

> The visual reference is **portrait and technical study as an editorial rhythm**, not a collage. A portrait, a product render, and a watch record must remain distinct unless a source specifically verifies their relationship.
