DiW (Designa Individual Watches) transforms the Rolex Daytona into one of the lightest chronographs in existence — a full NTPT carbon fibre case built around the genuine Rolex Caliber 4130 movement, worn on a handmade NATO-nylon strap. The 'Motley' edition carries a hand-finished paint-splash dial in a riot of colour against black carbon, limited to just five pieces worldwide.
