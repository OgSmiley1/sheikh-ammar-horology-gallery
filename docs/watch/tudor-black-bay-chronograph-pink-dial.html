Tudor Black Bay Chronograph 'Pink Dial' Inter Miami Edition

R · Limited

MovementAutomatic, Caliber MT5813 (COSC certified)

ComplicationsChronograph, Date
Year2024
ValuationUS$ 7,500

The pink dial came from Tudor's collaboration with Inter Miami FC — a football club co-owned by David Beckham. The striking pink color nodded to the team's iconic colors and immediately became one of the most talked-about watch releases of 2024. This unique colorway makes it instantly recognizable and highly collectible.
