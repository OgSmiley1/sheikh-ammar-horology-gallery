The F.P. Journe Tourbillon Souverain is François-Paul Journe's magnum opus — the watch that established his reputation as one of the world's greatest independent watchmakers. The tourbillon mechanism, originally invented by Abraham-Louis Breguet to combat the effects of gravity on movement accuracy, is here elevated by Journe's singular genius. This platinum-cased variant with a rare mint-inspired dial represents one of the most desirable and collectible expressions of the reference, combining exceptional mechanical artistry with a visually captivating colour that is unique among fine watchmaking.
