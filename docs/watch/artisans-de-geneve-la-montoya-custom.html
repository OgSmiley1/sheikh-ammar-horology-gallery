Artisans de Geneve created this exceptional timepiece in collaboration with F1 legend Juan Pablo Montoya. The watch features a completely reworked Rolex movement with custom-engraved bridges, a unique enamel dial depicting Montoya's most famous racing moments, and the signature Artisans de Geneve finishing that transforms it from a mere watch into a wearable piece of racing history.
