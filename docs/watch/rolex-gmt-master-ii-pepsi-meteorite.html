Combining the modern GMT-Master II with a genuine meteorite dial is the ultimate collector's statement. The Gibeon meteorite material, formed billions of years ago, creates a unique Widmanstätten pattern that can never be replicated. Paired with the white gold 'Pepsi' bezel, this is one of the most extraordinary watches in Sheikh Ammar's collection.
