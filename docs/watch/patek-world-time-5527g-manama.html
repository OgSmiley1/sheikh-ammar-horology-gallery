Patek Philippe World Time 5527G Manama

RR · Very Rare

MovementSelf-winding, Caliber 240 HU

ValuationUS$ 250,000

The Patek Philippe World Time Manama edition celebrates the beauty of Bahrain's capital city. The cloisonné enamel dial meticulously depicts the Manama skyline, with the World Time complication displaying all 24 time zones simultaneously. A unique tribute to the Gulf region from the master watchmakers of Geneva.
