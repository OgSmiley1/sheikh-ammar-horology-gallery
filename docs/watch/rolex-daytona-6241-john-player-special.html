Rolex · 6241 John Player Special

Rolex Daytona 6241 'John Player Special'

RRR · Extremely Rare

MovementManual-winding, Caliber 722-1 (Valjoux)
Case18K Yellow Gold
DialBlack with Gold Subdials
Case size37mm
Power reserve48 hours
ComplicationsChronograph, Tachymeter
Year1972
ValuationUS$ 180,000

The 6241 'John Player Special' is one of the most dramatic looking vintage Daytonas. The striking combination of black dial, gold sub-dials, and the yellow gold case creates an unmistakable aesthetic that collectors have nicknamed after the iconic black and gold John Player Special Formula 1 livery.
