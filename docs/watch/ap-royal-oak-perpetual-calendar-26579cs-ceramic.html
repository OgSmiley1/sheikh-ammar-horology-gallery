The Royal Oak Perpetual Calendar 26579CS in blue ceramic represents a bold evolution of the iconic Royal Oak design. Crafted entirely in blue ceramic, it features the perpetual calendar — one of watchmaking's grand complications — that accurately tracks the date, day, month, leap year, and moon phase without manual adjustment until 2100.
