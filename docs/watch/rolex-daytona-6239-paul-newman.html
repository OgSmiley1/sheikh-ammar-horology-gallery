Rolex Daytona 6239 Paul Newman

RRR · Extremely Rare

MovementManual-winding, Caliber 722 (Valjoux)
CaseStainless Steel
DialBlack Exotic with White Subdials (Paul Newman)
Case size37mm
Power reserve48 hours
ComplicationsChronograph, Tachymeter
Year1963
ValuationUS$ 350,000

Reference 6239 is the original Paul Newman Daytona — the watch that became one of the most famous timepieces in the world after Paul Newman's own 6239 was sold at auction for $17.75 million in 2017. The exotic dial with its distinctive Art Deco typeface and contrasting sub-dials created a collector's legend.
