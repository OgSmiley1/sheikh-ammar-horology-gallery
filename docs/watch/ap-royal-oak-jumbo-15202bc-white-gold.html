Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Jumbo Extra-Thin 15202BC White Gold

ValuationUS$ 150,000

The Royal Oak Jumbo 15202 is the direct descendant of the original 1972 Royal Oak. In 18K white gold, this 39mm timepiece maintains the purity of Gérald Genta's revolutionary design. The 'Jumbo' moniker refers to the extra-thin profile, with the movement measuring just 3.05mm in thickness.
