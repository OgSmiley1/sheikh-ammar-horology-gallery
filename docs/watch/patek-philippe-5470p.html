Patek Philippe 5470P Chronograph

ValuationUS$ 500,000

The Patek Philippe 5470P-001 is a groundbreaking chronograph that measures time to 1/10th of a second. Crafted in platinum, this watch features a stunning blue dial with a unique sector design paying homage to vintage chronographs. The monopusher chronograph activated by a single button demonstrates Patek Philippe's mastery of complex mechanisms.
