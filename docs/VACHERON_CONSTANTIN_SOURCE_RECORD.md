## Officially stated maison context

Vacheron Constantin identifies Geneva and 1755 as the beginning of its manufacture, and states that it has produced watches uninterruptedly since that year. The official manufacture page characterises its work through tradition and innovation, creativity, and a human-centred perspective. [1]

| Geneva | A geographic and cultural point of reference for the maison. |
| Craftsmanship | A lens through which to read published construction or finishing details only. |
