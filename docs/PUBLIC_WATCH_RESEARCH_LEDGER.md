## Research rules

> **Appearance evidence is not a complete-inventory or ownership claim.** A post or article can support a narrowly worded “publicly reported appearance” only when its date, context, image, and model identity remain traceable.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Permission-safe** | The original or a documented reusable asset is available for the intended archive use. |
| **Attribution-only** | The source can be linked but its image cannot be copied into the website. |
| **Corroboration pending** | A named reference requires primary-manufacturer or reputable specialist confirmation. |
