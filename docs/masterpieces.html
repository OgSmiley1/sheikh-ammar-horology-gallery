Catalogue · Selected Lots

Masterpieces of the Collection

Six pieces presented as they deserve — not as products, but as lots in a private catalogue, each with its own provenance.

Lot I

Rolex Daytona 6263 'Quraysh'

A Daytona bearing the Quraysh hawk — commissioned for the Arabian royal courts, among the rarest of all royal Rolexes.

The Rolex Daytona 6263 'Quraysh' is one of the most prized vintage Daytona variants. The distinctive 'step' between the bezel and case creates a unique profile that collectors worldwide seek. The yellow gold case with a black dial makes this a true collector's grail piece.

Year · 1984 Valuation · US$ 220,000

Lot II

Richard Mille RM 68-01 Cyril Kongo

One of thirty pieces worldwide — the dial graffitied by hand, one paint drop at a time, by artist Cyril Kongo.

The RM 68-01 Cyril Kongo is a wearable work of art. French street artist Cyril Kongo hand-painted the case bridges with his signature multicolor graffiti style, transforming the watch into a unique piece of contemporary art. With only 30 pieces ever made, it is among the rarest Richard Mille references.

Year · 2020 Valuation · US$ 2,500,000
Open the folio
Lot III

F.P. Journe Chronomètre à Résonance

Two beating hearts, synchronised by resonance alone — Journe’s answer to a problem posed in the eighteenth century.

This exceptional timepiece represents F.P. Journe's mastery of resonance — a phenomenon exploited to synchronize two independent oscillators, dramatically improving timekeeping stability. Limited to just 300 pieces worldwide, it is among the most coveted complications in independent watchmaking.

Year · 2000 Valuation · US$ 340,000
Open the folio
Lot IV

Richard Mille RM 26-02 Tourbillon Evil Eye

A tourbillon guarded by the evil eye — aerospace engineering rendered as talisman.

The Richard Mille RM 26-02 'Evil Eye' is one of the most extraordinary timepieces in Sheikh Ammar's collection. Featuring a hand-painted enamel dial depicting the traditional 'Evil Eye' talisman, the watch is limited to 30 pieces. The manual-winding tourbillon movement showcases the brand's technical prowess while the vibrant colors make each piece a unique work of art.

Year · 2016 Valuation · US$ 950,000
Open the folio
Lot V

Rolex Daytona 'Paul Newman' Reference 6265

The exotic dial collectors spent decades chasing — worn into legend by the man whose name it now carries.

The 6265 'Paul Newman' represents the pinnacle of vintage Rolex collecting. Named after the iconic actor and racing driver who famously wore a Daytona, this yellow gold example with the exotic 'Paul Newman' dial features contrasting sub-dial colors and Art Deco-inspired typography that makes it one of the most recognizable watch dials in the world.

Year · 1987 Valuation · US$ 450,000
Open the folio
Lot VI

Patek Philippe Nautilus 5711/1300A Olive Green Diamond

The final chapter of the 5711 — olive green, ringed in baguette diamonds, closed forever in 2021.

The olive green dial Nautilus represents the ultimate in rarity. As the final iteration of the legendary 5711 reference before its discontinuation, it combines a stunning olive-green dial with 32 baguette-cut diamonds on the bezel. Sheikh Ammar's example showcases his appreciation for both classic design and exceptional rarity.

Year · 2021 Valuation · US$ 525,000
