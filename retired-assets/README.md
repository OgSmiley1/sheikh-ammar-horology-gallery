# Retired assets — do not restore to `docs/`

## `films-ripped-social-clips/`

Four clips previously used as the homepage video hero and as ambient banners on
`patron.html` and `masterpieces.html`. They were described in CLAUDE.md as
"produced films." They are not. Each is a ripped 50/50 split-screen
watch-spotter reel: a candid photograph on the left, a stock watch render on the
right, divided by a black seam.

Specific problems, any one of which disqualifies them from a Crown Prince's gallery:

- `film-resonance` carries a burned-in third-party watermark (IFL Watches).
- `film-manama` shows an identifiable child.
- `film-kongo` does not appear to depict H.H. Sheikh Ammar at all.
- All four crop a candid photograph of the patron as background texture, with
  the live dial and the page headline laid over his face.

The hero and both banners now use the collection's own studio photography.
Kept here rather than deleted so the originals are recoverable if the owner
holds rights or wants them re-cut.
